# CareerOS

> An open-source career operating system for software engineers, AI engineers, and computer science students.

CareerOS is a structured framework for organizing, optimizing, and automating every stage of the technical job search.

Instead of relying on scattered notes, prompts, spreadsheets, and bookmarks, CareerOS provides one centralized system for:

- LinkedIn optimization
- ATS resume optimization
- Job discovery
- Company research
- Networking
- Interview preparation
- Portfolio strategy
- GitHub strategy
- Career documentation
- AI-powered workflows

---

# Why CareerOS?

Most job seekers work reactively.

They:

- Apply randomly
- Rewrite resumes from scratch
- Forget interview notes
- Lose recruiter contacts
- Miss follow-ups
- Repeat the same mistakes

CareerOS introduces an engineering approach to career management.

Treat your career like a software project.

Document it.

Measure it.

Improve it.

Automate repetitive tasks.

---

# Vision

To become the most comprehensive open-source framework for managing a software engineering career.

CareerOS combines proven career strategies with modern AI workflows to help engineers spend less time on repetitive tasks and more time preparing for opportunities.

---

# Who is it for?

CareerOS is designed for:

- Computer Science students
- Graduate Software Engineers
- AI Engineers
- Machine Learning Engineers
- Backend Engineers
- Full Stack Engineers
- Career changers entering software engineering

---

# Features

## LinkedIn Optimization

Improve recruiter visibility through profile optimization, keyword analysis, and positioning.

## Resume Intelligence

Tailor resumes for ATS systems while preserving truthful accomplishments.

## Job Intelligence

Collect, rank, and prioritize opportunities based on your experience and goals.

## Networking

Create repeatable workflows for recruiter outreach, referrals, and follow-ups.

## Interview Intelligence

Prepare for technical interviews using company-specific research and personalized study plans.

## Portfolio Strategy

Present projects effectively with clear documentation and measurable impact.

## Career Tracking

Track applications, interviews, recruiter conversations, and career progress.

## AI Workflows

Use AI responsibly to automate repetitive career tasks while keeping human judgment in control.
