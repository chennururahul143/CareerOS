# LinkedIn Profile Audit Prompt

## Purpose

Audit a LinkedIn profile against current AI Engineer and Software Engineer hiring standards.

---

## Input

- LinkedIn PDF
- Target role
- Target companies (optional)

---

## Expected Output

- Recruiter Score
- ATS Score
- Keyword Analysis
- Missing Skills
- Rewritten Headline
- Rewritten About
- Improvement Plan

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as a Senior LinkedIn Recruiter, ATS parser, AI Engineering Hiring Manager, and Personal Branding Consultant.

Audit my LinkedIn profile against 25+ recent AI Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, and Software Engineer job descriptions from leading technology companies.

Evaluate:

1. Headline
2. About Section
3. Experience
4. Projects
5. Skills
6. Featured Section
7. Certifications
8. Education
9. Recruiter SEO

Return:

• Recruiter Score (/10)
• ATS Score (/10)
• AI Engineer Score (/10)
• Software Engineer Score (/10)

Then:

- Identify every weakness.
- Explain why it matters.
- Rewrite each section.
- Suggest measurable improvements.
- Produce a prioritized action plan.
```

---

## Best Practices

- Compare against multiple job descriptions.
- Keep recommendations truthful.
- Prioritize measurable achievements.

---

## Limitations

- Depends on the quality of the LinkedIn profile.
- Cannot evaluate private profile settings.
