# Job Search Playbook

## Goal

Build a repeatable, data-driven job search system that helps software engineers and AI engineers identify high-quality opportunities, prioritize applications, prepare effectively, and maximize interview success.

---

# Target Audience

- Computer Science Students
- Graduate Software Engineers
- AI Engineers
- Machine Learning Engineers
- Full Stack Developers
- Career Changers

---

# Why Job Search Matters

Most candidates apply randomly.

CareerOS treats job searching as an engineering workflow.

Benefits:

- Higher interview rate
- Better resume targeting
- Better recruiter engagement
- Less wasted time
- Consistent tracking

---

# Workflow

```
Define Target Roles
        ↓
Find Jobs
        ↓
Rank Opportunities
        ↓
Research Company
        ↓
Tailor Resume
        ↓
Optimize LinkedIn
        ↓
Apply
        ↓
Contact Recruiter
        ↓
Prepare Interview
        ↓
Track Progress
```

---

# Step 1 — Define Target Roles

Example:

- AI Engineer
- Applied AI Engineer
- Machine Learning Engineer
- Software Engineer
- Backend Engineer

---

# Step 2 — Find Jobs

Sources

- LinkedIn
- Company Careers
- Wellfound
- Otta
- Indeed
- IrishJobs
- GradIreland

---

# Step 3 — Rank Opportunities

Score each job based on:

- Skills Match
- Tech Stack
- Location
- Career Growth
- Company Size
- Recruiter Availability
- Visa Sponsorship
- Salary (if available)

Prioritize jobs with the highest overall score.

---

# Step 4 — Company Research

Research:

- Products
- Technology Stack
- AI Initiatives
- Engineering Culture
- Recent News
- Interview Process

---

# Step 5 — Resume Optimization

Customize your resume for every application.

Verify:

- ATS keywords
- Relevant projects
- Technical skills
- Summary
- Experience

---

# Step 6 — LinkedIn Optimization

Ensure consistency between:

- Resume
- LinkedIn
- Portfolio
- GitHub

---

# Step 7 — Apply

Preferred order:

1. Company Careers Page
2. LinkedIn
3. Referral

Track every application.

---

# Step 8 — Recruiter Outreach

After applying:

- Connect on LinkedIn
- Send a short professional message
- Follow up after 5–7 business days if appropriate

---

# Step 9 — Interview Preparation

Generate:

- Company research
- Technical questions
- Behavioral questions
- System Design topics
- AI/ML topics
- Questions to ask the interviewer

---

# Step 10 — Track Progress

Track:

- Company
- Role
- Date Applied
- Resume Version
- Recruiter
- Status
- Follow-up Date
- Interview Notes
- Outcome

---

# AI Prompt

## Intelligent Job Search & Ranking Prompt

```text
Act as an AI Career Coach, Technical Recruiter, Hiring Manager, and Job Market Analyst.

Help me build a prioritized job application pipeline.

Step 1
Find relevant jobs matching my background.

Target roles:

- AI Engineer
- Applied AI Engineer
- Machine Learning Engineer
- Software Engineer
- Backend Engineer

Prioritize jobs posted within the last 7 days.

Step 2

For every job evaluate:

- Skills Match
- Experience Match
- Tech Stack Match
- Education Match
- Location
- Visa Sponsorship
- Company Reputation
- Career Growth

Assign an Overall Fit Score (/10).

Step 3

Rank every opportunity from highest to lowest priority.

Step 4

For the Top 10 jobs provide:

- Company
- Job Title
- Location
- Why it matches my profile
- Missing Skills
- Resume Changes Needed
- Recruiter Outreach Recommendation

Step 5

Generate:

- Company research checklist
- Interview preparation checklist
- Application checklist

Finally produce today's application priority list.
```

# Common Mistakes

- Applying without tailoring a resume
- Ignoring company research
- Applying to old listings
- Never following up
- Applying to too many unrelated roles
- Not tracking applications

- Resume optimized
- LinkedIn aligned
- Company researched
- Recruiter contacted
- Application submitted
- Interview preparation started
- Application logged
