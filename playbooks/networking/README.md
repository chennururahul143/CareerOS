# Networking Playbook

## Goal

Build meaningful professional relationships that increase opportunities for referrals, interviews, mentorship, and career growth.

---

# Target Audience

- Students
- Graduate Engineers
- Software Engineers
- AI Engineers
- Career Changers

---

# Why Networking Matters

Many technical roles are filled through referrals and professional relationships before reaching large applicant pools.

Networking is about building genuine connections, not sending mass messages.

---

# Workflow

```
Identify Target Company
        ↓
Find Relevant People
        ↓
Research Their Background
        ↓
Send Personalized Connection Request
        ↓
Build Conversation
        ↓
Ask Relevant Questions
        ↓
Request Referral (If Appropriate)
        ↓
Maintain Relationship
```

---

# Step 1 — Identify Target Companies

Focus on companies aligned with your goals.

Examples:

- Startups
- Scale-ups
- Enterprise
- Research Labs

---

# Step 2 — Find the Right People

Prioritize:

- Recruiters
- Engineering Managers
- Senior Engineers
- Alumni
- Hiring Managers

---

# Step 3 — Research

Before reaching out, understand:

- Current role
- Recent posts
- Shared interests
- Company

---

# Step 4 — Connection Request

Keep it short.

Example:

Hi [Name],

I came across your profile while researching opportunities at [Company]. I'm currently pursuing an MSc in Computer Science and building AI applications. I'd love to connect and learn from your experience.

Thank you.

---

# Step 5 — Build the Relationship

Don't immediately ask for a referral.

Instead:

- Ask thoughtful questions.
- Engage with posts.
- Share relevant work.
- Stay professional.

---

# Step 6 — Referral Request

Only ask after building rapport.

Be respectful if they decline.

---

# Step 7 — Follow Up

Follow up once after about a week if appropriate.

Avoid repeated messages.

# Checklist

- Company identified
- Right people found
- Profile researched
- Connection request sent
- Conversation started
- Follow-up completed
- Referral requested (if appropriate)

---

# Common Mistakes

- Asking for referrals immediately
- Sending generic messages
- Spamming recruiters
- Ignoring follow-ups
- Treating networking as a one-time task

---

# Definition of Done

- Professional connection established
- Conversation initiated
- Relationship maintained
- Referral opportunity identified
- Follow-up tracked
