# CareerOS Roadmap

## Vision

CareerOS aims to become the definitive open-source operating system for software engineers, AI engineers, and computer science students managing their careers.

Rather than offering isolated prompts or templates, CareerOS provides structured workflows, reusable resources, and automation that support every stage of the technical job search.

---

# Mission

Help engineers spend less time managing career logistics and more time building skills, preparing for interviews, and securing opportunities.

---

# Core Principles

CareerOS is built around five engineering principles.

- Systematic over random
- Document everything
- Automate repetitive work
- Measure outcomes
- Continuously improve

## Phase 1 — Career Strategy

Objective

Document proven strategies for technical job searching.

Modules

- LinkedIn Strategy
- Resume Strategy
- GitHub Strategy

- Interview Strategy

Success Criteria
