# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in CareerOS.

Whether you are contributing documentation, workflows, templates, automation, or ideas, we expect all interactions to remain professional and constructive.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful during discussions.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Helping others learn.
- Sharing knowledge openly.
- Respecting different backgrounds and experiences.
- Focusing on solving problems.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Trolling or inflammatory comments.
- Publishing misleading or deceptive career advice.
- Encouraging dishonest practices such as fake resumes or fabricated experience.

---

# Project Values

CareerOS promotes:

- Integrity
- Transparency
- Continuous learning
- Professional growth
- Ethical use of AI

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove contributions that violate these principles.

Repeated violations may result in restricted participation.

---

# Reporting

If you encounter inappropriate behavior, please open a private issue or contact the maintainers.

All reports will be handled respectfully and confidentially.

---

Thank you for helping build a professional community around CareerOS.
